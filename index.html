❓ FAQ & Allotment Logic

Q: How does this tool estimate seat allotments? It matches your All India Rank (AIR) or State Serial Number (S.No) against official college closing ranks. It incorporates the +810 newly added MBBS seats in 2026 (+110 Govt + +700 Private Cat-A) and applies category reservation rules (OC, EWS, BC-A to E, SC1-SC3, ST).
Q: What official cutoff data was used to build this tool? Last year rank based and final rank of SC2 was considered for developing this one which was after Round 3 counselling. Extracted directly from KNRUHS Round 3 Cutoffs (TS MBBS-College Wise Cut off (OU) ROUND-3.pdf) and Final State Merit List (last_year_merit_list_final_tg.pdf).
Q: What were the exact Round 3 closing cutoffs for SC2? • Government Colleges: Closed at SC2 Rank #399 (AIR 260,591 / 375 Marks - GMC Mulugu).
• Private Colleges (Cat-A): Closed at SC2 Rank #607 (AIR 312,829 / 351 Marks - Nova / Surabhi / TRR).
• With +810 seats added in 2026, Private SC2 cutoffs expanded to SC2 Rank #641 (AIR ~335,000).
Q: If a college does not show as allotted here, does that mean I won't get it? No, not at all! This is a simulation tool. Vacancies open up in Mop-Up and Stray Vacancy rounds when candidates move to AIQ/AIIMS. Always fill all your preferred Government and Private colleges during official KNRUHS web options counselling!
