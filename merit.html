Telangana MBBS Reservation & Quota Details

📋 Official KNRUHS 3-Phase Allotment Rules

1. Phase 1: Open Category Allotment - All seats under Open Category (Unreserved) are allotted strictly by General State Merit without consuming reserved category quotas.
2. Phase 2: MRC Sliding (Rule 10(2)) - Reserved candidates who occupy Open Category seats and can get a higher-rated college using their category quota are re-allotted under MRC. Their vacated seat is converted to their reserved category.
3. Phase 3: Category Allotment - Remaining candidates are allotted category-wise into reserved seats and converted seats.
